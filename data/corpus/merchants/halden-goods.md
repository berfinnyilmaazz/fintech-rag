# Merchant Profile: Halden Goods

Halden Goods is a secondhand furniture marketplace with pickup and home transport, based in Sweden.

- **Live since:** 2026-01
- **Settlement currency:** SEK
- **MCC:** 5712
- **Account manager:** Sara Lind

## Integration and terms
High average order value. Payment is authorised at order and captured at pickup, so authorisations older than 7 days must be re-created. Sellers are mostly private individuals with light KYB.

Monthly settlement reports for Halden Goods are published under `settlement-reports/`.
