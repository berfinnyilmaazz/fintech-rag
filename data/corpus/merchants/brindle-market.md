# Merchant Profile: Brindle Market

Brindle Market is a home goods marketplace with about 3,000 independent sellers, based in Germany.

- **Live since:** 2025-11
- **Settlement currency:** EUR
- **MCC:** 5719
- **Account manager:** Ines Kohl

## Integration and terms
Standard T+2 payouts. Integrated on Payments API v2 since onboarding. Uses manual capture, capturing when the seller confirms dispatch.

Monthly settlement reports for Brindle Market are published under `settlement-reports/`.
