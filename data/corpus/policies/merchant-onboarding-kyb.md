# Merchant Onboarding (KYB)

Every merchant and every marketplace seller passes know-your-business verification before receiving payouts.

## Required for merchants
- Company registration extract less than 3 months old.
- Ultimate beneficial owners holding 25 percent or more, with identity documents.
- A bank account in the merchant's own name, verified by a 0.01 EUR test deposit.
- Expected monthly volume and the business's MCC.

## Required for sellers
Sellers on a marketplace provide a legal name, country, date of birth or registration number, and a bank account. Sellers with more than 15,000 EUR monthly volume get full KYB.

## Decisions
Risk & Compliance approves, rejects or requests more information within 3 working days. Merchants in high-risk MCCs, such as 5967 or 7995, need a second approver.

Payments can be accepted before KYB completes, but payouts stay on hold until approval.
